How to add a lamp

Follow this guide to connect a new WLED lamp to Glow.

1. Power on your WLED lamp

Switch on your lamp and wait about a minute for it to start. If it has not been set up before, it will usually create its own Wi-Fi network called WLED-AP.

2. Connect your phone to WLED-AP

Open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and look for WLED-AP. Tap that network to connect to your lamp.

If your phone asks whether to stay connected because the network has no internet, choose the option that keeps you connected to WLED-AP.

3. Open the WLED page

Your phone may show a Wi-Fi notification or sign-in prompt after connecting. Tap it to open the WLED page.

If nothing appears, open your phone's browser and enter this address:

4. Open Config and Wi-Fi Setup

On the WLED control screen, tap Config in the top-right corner. Then select Wi-Fi Setup.

5. Enter your home Wi-Fi details

Enter your normal home Wi-Fi network name and password. These are the details your phone uses to connect to your home Wi-Fi.

Tap Save & Connect. Your lamp will try to join your home Wi-Fi and may restart. Your phone may disconnect from WLED-AP during this step.

Important: reconnect your phone to your normal home Wi-Fi before continuing. If the WLED page no longer opens, you may need to find the lamp's address using your router's connected-device list before opening WLED again.

6. Find the lamp's Client IP address

Once you can access the WLED control page on your home network, tap Config, then Wi-Fi Setup. Look for Client IP. This is the address Glow needs.

Copy the Client IP shown on your own controller. 192.168.1.175 is only an example; your lamp's address will probably be different.

7. Add your lamp to Glow

Return to Glow and open My Devices. Tap Add New Device, enter a name for your lamp and enter its Client IP address. Save the device, then select it to test the connection.

Quick checklist
✓ Phone connected to home Wi-Fi
✓ Lamp connected to the same home network
✓ Correct Client IP copied
✓ Lamp saved in Glow

Tip: Your phone and WLED lamp normally need to be on the same home network for Glow to control the lamp.
